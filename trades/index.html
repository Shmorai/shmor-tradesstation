Not sure which trade you need?

  • More than one trade involved? Start with a general builder who can manage the project.
  • Adding space? See extensions and loft conversions.
  • New kitchen or bathroom? Talk to kitchen fitters or bathroom fitters, who will bring in an electrician or gas engineer where needed.
  • Leak or storm damage? Call a roofer.
  • Finishing touches? Plasterers, decorators and carpenters.
