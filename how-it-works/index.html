How it works

How BuildersUK works

BuildersUK is a free guide and introduction service for homeowners. We help you understand your job, then point you to trusted quote platforms so you can hear from local tradespeople.

Photo: immo RENOVATION on Unsplash

Three simple steps

  1. Find your trade

    Pick the trade you need and read what to check before you hire.

  2. Describe your job

    Request quotes through our partner platforms with one simple description.

  3. Compare and hire

    Compare tradespeople, check them, and agree the job directly.

Is BuildersUK free?

Yes. Using BuildersUK and requesting quotes through our partners is free for homeowners, with no obligation to hire.

How we make money

Some links on BuildersUK are referral links. When we introduce you to a partner platform, that partner may pay us a fee. This never changes the price you pay a tradesperson. Read more about how we make money.

Who are the tradespeople?

Tradespeople are independent businesses listed with our partner platforms. BuildersUK does not employ, endorse or vet individual tradespeople, so always make your own checks. Our hiring guide explains how.
