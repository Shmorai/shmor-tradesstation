What a painter and decorator can help with

  • Interior walls, ceilings and woodwork
  • Exterior masonry, windows and doors
  • Wallpaper hanging and removal
  • Feature walls and specialist finishes
  • Preparation: filling, sanding and priming

When do you need one?

  • You are moving in or getting ready to sell
  • You have had plastering or building work done
  • Exterior paintwork is peeling or weathered

Checks before you hire

  • Agree the preparation: much of a good finish comes from filling, sanding and priming.
  • Who buys the paint, which brand, and how many coats are included.
  • Membership of a trade body such as the Painting and Decorating Association (PDA) is a good sign.
  • Protection of floors and furniture, and clearing up each day.

What affects the cost?

Prices vary from job to job. The main factors are:

  • Number and size of rooms
  • Condition of surfaces and amount of preparation
  • Woodwork, doors and ceilings included
  • Exterior access and scaffolding
  • Paint and wallpaper quality

Every job is different, so the only reliable way to know the price for your home is to get at least three written, itemised quotes for the same scope of work and compare them like for like.

Frequently asked questions

Do decorators supply the paint?
Some include it in the price, others ask you to buy it. Agree the brand, colour and finish in writing before work starts.
How long should I wait to paint new plaster?
New plaster needs to dry fully and usually needs a mist coat (thinned paint) first. Ask your plasterer and decorator for advice on your walls.
Can a decorator paint the outside of my house?
Yes, many do. For work at height they will need ladders, towers or scaffolding, which can affect the price.
