  • Kitchens and bathrooms: kitchen fitters and bathroom fitters.
  • Outside the house: roofers and landscapers for patios, driveways and fencing.
  • Inside the house: electricians, plasterers, painters and decorators and carpenters and joiners.

Hire with confidence

  • Get three written quotes for the same job.
  • Check registrations where the law requires them: Gas Safe for gas work, competent person schemes for electrics.
  • Look for trade body membership such as the FMB or TrustMark, and check it on their website.
  • Agree a written contract and pay in stages, never a large sum upfront.

Frequently asked questions

Is BuildersUK free to use?
Yes. BuildersUK is free for homeowners, and requesting quotes through our partners comes with no obligation to hire.
How does BuildersUK make money?
Some links are referral links. When we introduce you to a partner platform, the partner may pay us a fee. This never changes what you pay.
Does BuildersUK vet tradespeople?
No. Tradespeople are independent businesses listed with our partner platforms. Always check registrations, insurance, reviews and references before you hire. Our hiring guide shows you how.
Which areas do you cover?
The whole of the UK. Some rules, such as planning and Building Regulations, differ between England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, and our guides point this out.
