Garage conversions: planning, regulations and tips

An integral or attached garage can become a home office, playroom, bedroom or utility room without building an extension. Here is what permission you need and what the work involves.

By the TradesStation editorial team · 14 October 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: GoodLifeConstruction on Unsplash

On this page

  1. Planning permission
  2. Building Regulations
  3. Things to plan for
  4. Next steps

Planning permission

Converting a garage usually does not need planning permission, as long as the work is internal and does not enlarge the building. Check first if:

  • your home is on a newer estate, where permitted development rights for garage conversions are sometimes removed by a planning condition;
  • you live in a conservation area or a listed building;
  • you want to create a separate home, such as an annexe to rent out; or
  • you live in a flat or maisonette.

Building Regulations

A garage conversion will normally need Building Regulations approval, because a garage is not built to the same standard as a habitable room. Typical areas include:

  • Filling in the garage door opening with a new wall, with suitable foundations.
  • Insulating the floor, walls and ceiling, and dealing with damp.
  • Ventilation and windows.
  • Fire safety, including escape routes.
  • Electrics, heating and any drainage.

Things to plan for

  • Floor level: garage floors are often lower than the house, so the floor may need raising.
  • Parking: some councils and estates have rules about keeping off-street parking. Check before you start.
  • The front of the house: choose a new window and wall finish that matches the rest of the house.
  • Storage: think about where bikes, tools and garden equipment will go.

Next steps

Ask a builder who has done garage conversions before to look at your garage, and agree who will deal with Building Control. See also what to include in a builder contract.

Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own building rules. This guide covers England; elsewhere in the UK, check with your local council.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission to convert my garage?
Usually not, if the work is internal and does not enlarge the building. Check whether permitted development rights have been removed on your property, and whether it is listed or in a conservation area.
Does a garage conversion need Building Regulations approval?
Yes, normally. Converting a garage into a habitable room involves the structure, insulation, ventilation, fire safety and electrics.
