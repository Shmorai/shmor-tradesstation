Fuse box upgrades and signs you need a rewire

Your consumer unit (fuse box) is the heart of your home’s electrics. Here is when it is worth upgrading, the signs your wiring may need replacing, and how to make sure the work is certified.

By the TradesStation editorial team · 14 October 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: mostafa mahmoudi on Unsplash

On this page

  1. Why consumer units get upgraded
  2. Signs you may need a rewire
  3. The rules
  4. What you should receive
  5. Planning a rewire

Why consumer units get upgraded

  • Old rewireable fuses (with fuse wire) or a wooden-backed board are signs of an old installation.
  • RCD protection: modern consumer units include residual current devices that cut the power quickly if there is an earth fault, reducing the risk of a fatal electric shock. Older boards often have limited or no RCD protection.
  • Fire safety: current wiring standards require consumer units in homes to have a non-combustible enclosure, typically metal.
  • New circuits: an EV charger, electric shower or extension may need spare capacity or a new board.

Signs you may need a rewire

  • Old cable types, such as rubber or fabric-insulated cable, or lead-sheathed cable.
  • Round-pin sockets, or sockets and switches mounted on skirting boards.
  • Frequent trips or blown fuses, flickering lights, or scorch marks and burning smells at sockets.
  • Not enough sockets, with extension leads everywhere.
  • An EICR that recommends significant remedial work.

Only an electrician can tell you for sure. An EICR is the usual starting point.

The rules

In England, replacing a consumer unit and installing new circuits are notifiable under Part P of the Building Regulations. Use an electrician registered with a competent person scheme so they can self-certify; otherwise the work must be inspected by Building Control or a third-party certifier.

What you should receive

  • An Electrical Installation Certificate for the new consumer unit or rewire.
  • A Building Regulations compliance certificate through the electrician’s scheme (or from Building Control).
  • Clear labelling of circuits on the consumer unit.

Planning a rewire

A full rewire usually means lifting floorboards and chasing cables into walls, so it is best done before decorating, ideally alongside other renovation work. Budget for plastering and making good afterwards. Compare local electricians.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a legal requirement to upgrade an old fuse box?
Not for owner-occupiers in itself, but an old board may be coded as needing improvement or as potentially dangerous on an EICR. Landlords must act on C1, C2 and FI observations.
Does replacing a consumer unit need Building Regulations approval?
Yes, it is notifiable work in England. A registered competent person can self-certify it, and you should receive a certificate.
